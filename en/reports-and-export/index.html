Services

Reports that send themselves

Nobody should spend a Friday pasting numbers together.

Many businesses spend hours each month assembling the same report. When the data is already gathered, the report can be created automatically and sent to the right people, as PDF or Excel.

Examples

  • A weekly report to management every Monday
  • A monthly report to the board and bank
  • Export to Excel for further analysis
  • Alerts when a key figure goes outside its limit

Relevant experience

  • Statistics with export to PDF and Excel (PopLeads, Turkey)
  • PDF document generation (CFN Teknoloji, Turkey)
  • Reports with IBM Cognos BI

Frequently asked questions

Can the report look like our current one?

Yes. We often start from the report you already make by hand.

Who receives the reports?

Those you choose. Recipients and permissions are set per report.
