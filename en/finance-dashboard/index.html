Finance every week, not every quarter

Financial decisions get better when the numbers are fresh.

The monthly report often arrives weeks after month-end. A finance dashboard reads straight from the books and shows profit, margin and liquidity continuously. I hold an ACCA qualification in accounting, so the numbers are calculated the right way.

Examples

  • Profit per month, department or project
  • Margin per product and customer
  • Costs against budget
  • Receivables and expected cash flow

Relevant experience

  • Finance dashboard in an ERP system (Samsara Solutions, Bulgaria)
  • Reports with IBM Cognos BI
  • ACCA Certified Accounting Technician (levels A and B)

Frequently asked questions

Does it replace year-end and audit?

No. It gives an ongoing overview; year-end is done as usual by your accounting firm.

Can the board get its own view?

Yes, with export to PDF or Excel before meetings.
