Inventory and purchasing ahead of time

See what is about to run out before it does, and what is standing still.

Inventory ties up capital. A dashboard shows what sells, what stands still and what will soon need ordering. With data from inventory and sales, the need is visible before it becomes a back order.

Examples

  • Stock below reorder point
  • Inventory value and turnover
  • Items with no movement in the last 90 days
  • Delivery precision per supplier

Relevant experience

  • Dashboards for purchasing and suppliers (ITG, Turkey)
  • Inventory with cost tracking and shrinkage in an ERP system (Samsara Solutions, Bulgaria)

Frequently asked questions

Can the dashboard suggest purchases?

Yes, simple suggestions based on sales rate and lead time. You make the decision.

Does it work with several warehouses?

Yes. Stock is shown per warehouse and in total.

More areas

  • Sales

    Sales per day, product and seller on one screen.

  • Finance and cash flow

    Profit, margin and cash flow every week.

  • Occupancy and bookings

    Bookings, cancellations and free slots in numbers.

  • Web and marketing

    Visits, enquiries and what brings customers.

  • Automatic reports

    A weekly report in the inbox, export to Excel and PDF.
