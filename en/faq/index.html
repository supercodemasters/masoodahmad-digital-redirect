FAQ

Short answers about dashboards and reports.

What does a dashboard cost?

It depends on the number of sources and views. You get a written proposal with a price after the first call.

Do you use Power BI?

When it fits. Sometimes a simple web dashboard is cheaper and faster; we choose by need.

How often are the numbers updated?

From instantly to once a night, depending on the source systems.

Who can see the dashboard?

Those you choose. Login and permissions control who sees what.

Shall we have a short call?

Book a short video or phone call. The first call is free of charge.
