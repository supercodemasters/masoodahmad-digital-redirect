Services

Sales on one screen

See how the week is going while there is still time to change it.

Sales figures often come from the till, the web shop and the invoices separately. A sales dashboard fetches every source automatically and shows the same number to everyone, every day.

Common views

  • Sales per day, week and month against last year
  • Best and worst products
  • Sales per channel: shop, web, sales staff
  • Average order and returning customers

Relevant experience

  • Google Analytics and Mixpanel dashboards (clients in the USA)
  • Dashboards for purchasing and suppliers (ITG, Turkey)

Frequently asked questions

Can the dashboard run on a TV in the office?

Yes. It runs in the browser on a computer, phone or a screen on site.

Where do the numbers come from?

Straight from the till, web shop or finance system via their APIs or export files.

More areas

  • Finance and cash flow

    Profit, margin and cash flow every week.

  • Occupancy and bookings

    Bookings, cancellations and free slots in numbers.

  • Inventory and purchasing

    Stock, inventory value and purchase needs ahead of time.

  • Web and marketing

    Visits, enquiries and what brings customers.

  • Automatic reports

    A weekly report in the inbox, export to Excel and PDF.
