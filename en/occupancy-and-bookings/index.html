Occupancy in real time

Free slots are lost income. See them in time.

For businesses that live on booked times, occupancy is the key figure. A dashboard shows how full each person, room or machine is, and where cancellations and no-shows cost most.

Common measures

  • Occupancy per resource and week
  • Cancellations and no-shows
  • Revenue per hour or per booking
  • Online bookings compared with phone bookings

Good to know

  • Data comes from your booking system
  • Works with both ready-made and custom booking systems
  • Personal data is not shown in the dashboard

Frequently asked questions

Does it work with our ready-made booking system?

If the system has an API or can export data, yes.

Can we compare periods?

Yes, for example the same week last year, or before and after a change.
